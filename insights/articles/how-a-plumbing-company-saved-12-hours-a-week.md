## The situation

A regional plumbing company — 15 employees, 3 trucks, solid reputation — came to us frustrated. The owner was working 60-hour weeks, mostly on admin. His team was great at plumbing. Everything else felt like chaos.

"I keep hearing I need AI," he said. "But I don't even know where to start."

## What we found

We started with a process audit — no technology, just observation and documentation. Here's what the first week revealed:

- **Dispatch was entirely manual.** The office manager juggled phone calls, a whiteboard, and text messages to route techs to jobs. Average time per dispatch: 12 minutes.
- **Invoicing happened at the end of each day.** Techs wrote job details on paper forms, brought them back to the office, and the bookkeeper entered them manually. Average lag: 2 days.
- **Follow-ups were inconsistent.** No system for checking back with customers after service. Some got calls, most didn't.

**Total admin overhead: roughly 28 hours per week across three people.**

## The fix

We didn't start with AI. We started with process design.

### Phase 1: Standardize (Week 1-2)
- Created a dispatch SOP with clear criteria for job prioritization
- Designed a simple mobile form for techs to submit job details from the field
- Built a follow-up checklist triggered by job completion

### Phase 2: Automate (Week 3-4)
- Implemented an online booking system that handled 60% of scheduling automatically
- Connected the mobile form to their invoicing software — eliminating manual entry
- Set up automated follow-up emails at 24 hours and 7 days post-service

### Phase 3: Evaluate AI (Week 5-6)
- Tested an AI call-routing tool for after-hours inquiries
- Evaluated AI-generated invoice descriptions (faster than manual, but needed review)
- **Decided against** an AI-powered CRM — the cost didn't justify the benefit at their scale

## The results

After six weeks:

- **12 hours per week saved** in admin time
- **Invoice turnaround dropped from 2 days to same-day**
- **Customer follow-up rate went from ~30% to 100%**
- **Monthly tool cost: $180** (scheduling + forms + email automation)

The owner's reaction: "I thought I needed some big AI thing. Turns out I just needed to see my own business clearly."

## The takeaway

The plumbing company's story is common. The bottleneck wasn't technology — it was visibility. Once they could see their processes clearly, the solutions were obvious. Some involved AI. Most didn't.

> The best technology investment is the one that solves a problem you've actually measured.
